A happy birthday mom video here is a 12-second vertical clip of your own mom at her birthday surprise, made from one photo of her: the cake set down in front of her, her hands over her mouth, the candles going out. ## How to make it

1. **Choose a photo where she looks like herself.** Her face toward the camera and easy to see. A selfie she sent you, a wedding photo, last summer at the beach: any of them works.
2. **Pick her surprise and make it hers.** Put her age on the candles, her favourite flowers in the bouquet, "Happy birthday, Mom" and her name in the line for who it's for.
3. **Send it with three lines from you.** Download the clip with no watermark, send it on WhatsApp or by text with a happy birthday message only you would write, or post it with the song she loves.
