## How to make it

1. **Upload one photo of your dog.** Face toward the camera, coat in good light, not half hidden behind a person or a blanket. A camera roll photo is fine.
2. **Pick the party.** The gift box, the party couch, the noisemaker wake-up, the cake smash, the goat at the cake.
3. **Write in your details.** Their age on the candle, their name on the cake, or who the clip is for if the dog is the one saying it.
4. **Download and send.** Save the happy birthday clip with no watermark, post it, or drop it in the family chat with the birthday song added in the app.
