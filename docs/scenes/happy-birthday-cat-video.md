A happy birthday cat video here is a 12-second vertical clip of your own cat at its birthday party, made from one photo of it. ## How to make it

1. **Upload a photo of your cat.** Face toward the camera, eyes open, the whole head in frame. A nap photo on the sofa works if you can see its markings.
2. **Pick the party and make it theirs.** Change the number candle to its age, the cake to a whole fish, or the banner to its name. Sending it from the cat? Write the person's name in the line for who it's for.
3. **Save it and send it.** Download the clip and post it as it is, or add the birthday song you want in the app you post from.
